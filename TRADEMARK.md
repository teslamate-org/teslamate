Last updated: September 23, 2026

## 1. Purpose

This policy exists to protect the integrity of TeslaMate and its community. It ensures that users can clearly distinguish the Software and the Project from unofficial, commercial, or potentially deceptive third-party offerings.

It exists alongside the Software’s [license](https://github.com/teslamate-org/teslamate/blob/main/LICENSE) (AGPL-3.0-or-later). The AGPL covers the code; this policy covers the branding. The AGPL is a copyright license and grants no rights under trademark law.

- **Name**: "TeslaMate" in any form and every other name that could be mistaken for it, including, without limitation, any spelling, capitalization, spacing, or hyphenation.

- **Marks**: the Name and the Logos, each on its own and in any combination.
- **Project**: the TeslaMate open-source project, represented by the `teslamate-org` GitHub organization and its maintainers.
- **Tool**: anything not published by the Project that is made to work with the Software, including, without limitation, apps, dashboards, integrations, scripts, and services.
- **Commercial**: in the course of trade or serving direct or indirect financial gain, regardless of the gain's amount or purpose and in whatever form it accrues, including, without limitation, sales, subscriptions, advertising, sponsorship, and donations.

The Marks are owned by the Project. The Name must always be written in CamelCase: TeslaMate (never "Teslamate", "teslamate", "tesla mate", "Tesla-Mate", or any other variation), except in a technical identifier that the Project uses or that Section 4 permits. The Project uses the lowercase form `teslamate` as a technical identifier, including, without limitation, its container image, package, and repository names; `teslamate-org` as its GitHub organization; and `teslamate.org` as its domain.

TeslaMate is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Tesla, Inc. "Tesla" and related marks are trademarks of Tesla, Inc.

## 3. Philosophy

TeslaMate is open source and encourages self-hosting.

We want individuals, communities, and non-profits to freely use the Marks for non-commercial purposes as permitted in Section 4.\
At the same time, we reserve the right to control every other use of the Marks, including, without limitation, commercial use and any use that could be mistaken for a use by the Project.

You may use the Marks without prior written consent only as follows. Each item states all of its conditions and applies only while all of them are met.

- Personal self-hosting: running the Software, modified or unmodified, non-commercially for vehicles used by you or your household, including under the identifiers the Software uses and under a hostname that contains the Name.
- Contributing: keeping a copy of any of the Project's repositories, locally or as a fork on a code hosting platform under the repository's original name, including the Logos it contains, for contributing to the Project or, non-commercially, for modifying it for use under the Personal self-hosting item.
  That copy may be run to develop and test contributions to the Project, but not for vehicles in regular use.
  This item applies only while the copy is presented only as a copy of that repository and publishes no artifacts.
- Tools: a non-commercial Tool that is open-source under an AGPL-compatible license and integrates with the Software only via the supported integration surface (MQTT) may describe in its descriptive text how it works with the Software, including, without limitation, by stating "compatible with TeslaMate", may be named "[own name] for TeslaMate" with an own name that could not be mistaken for the Name,
  may use the corresponding lowercase identifier "[own-name]-for-teslamate" as its technical identifier, and may use the identifiers of that integration surface, including its MQTT topics.

## 6. Requirements for Third-Party Projects

Every Tool must include a prominent disclaimer wherever it is presented to users, including, without limitation, its README, About section, and store or catalog listing.
This applies regardless of how the Tool integrates; MQTT is the only supported integration surface, and using an unsupported integration path does not exempt a Tool from this requirement.
The disclaimer must be reproduced verbatim; paraphrased or shortened versions do not satisfy this requirement.

"This project is an unofficial community tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by the official TeslaMate project."
